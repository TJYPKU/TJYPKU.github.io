Junyu Tang

Postdoctoral Fellow in Condensed Matter Theory

International Center for Quantum Materials, Peking University

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Peking University, working with Prof. Gang v. Chen. I received my Ph.D. in Physics from the University of California, Riverside, where I worked with Prof. Ran Cheng. My research focuses on quantum magnetism, spintronics, topological matter, and strongly correlated systems.

EmailGoogle ScholarORCIDCV

Research interests

  • Strongly correlated systems:
    Superconductivity, non-Abelian anyon, Chiral spin liquid, Mott insulators
  • Spintronics and Quantum magnetism:
    Spin-orbit torques, antiferromagnetic dynamics, spin-lattice coupling, flavor-wave theory
  • Topology in condensed matter:
    Topological magnetic materials, topological magnon
